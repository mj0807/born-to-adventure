GOOD TO KNOW

Before you book.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 98577 06000 with the activity, your preferred date and the number of guests. We'll confirm availability and share booking details. You can also call 8219050387 or 9857706000.

Is there a minimum age?

Paragliding and river rafting are for guests aged 12 and above. For other activities, message us with the guest's age and we'll advise.

Do prices change during peak season?

No. Our prices stay the same across all seasons.

Do you offer group or corporate discounts?

Yes. Tell us your group size and the activities you're interested in and we'll share a group price.

Is the booking amount refundable?

The booking amount is non-refundable. Please confirm your plans before paying it.

Which rafting route should I choose?

The 6 km route is a great introduction, while the 9 km and 14 km routes give you longer on the river. Prices start from ₹750 per person; ask us for the rate on your chosen route.
